# Agility courses and thieving

Checked against the server code (vendor/rs-sdk at 3506b20cc). Where this page disagrees with
wiki/skills/agility.md or wiki/skills/thieving.md, this page is correct.

## Agility course XP per lap

| Course | Lap bonus | Total per lap |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Gnome Stronghold | 39 | 86.5 |
| Barbarian Outpost | 42 | 139.5 (149.5 with the entry pipe) |
| Wilderness | 498.9 | 571.4 (586.4 with the entry ridge) |

- The wiki lap bonus (Barbarian 139.5, Wilderness 571.5) and total (222, 659) are wrong.
- A lap bonus never fails. It is given when you finish the last obstacle.
- Barbarian Outpost: only the entry pipe from the north checks a level (Agility 35). No other obstacle checks a level.
- Wilderness course: the entry ridge needs Agility 52, and the pipe needs Agility 49.
- The Taverley to Falador crumbling wall gives 12.5 XP, not 15. It is members-only.

## Thieving: what the wiki gets wrong

- All pickpocketing, stalls and chests are members-only.
- A farmer gives 9 coins only, no seeds.
- A hero gives 200 to 300 coins, 2 death runes, a jug of wine, 1 blood rune, a fire orb or gold ore. The only gem is a rare diamond.
- The 10 gp chest needs Thieving 13, not 1.
- The nature rune chest gives 1 nature rune and 3 coins, not 3 nature runes.
- The steel arrowtips chest is locked, not trapped. You need a lockpick. It also gives 20 coins.
- The blood rune chest also gives 500 coins and teleports you away.
- Stolen silk sells to the Silk merchant in East Ardougne, not the silk trader. 60 gp needs haggling. He calls the guards if you stole silk recently.

## Thieving targets the wiki leaves out

Rogue 32, Fremennik 45 (needs The Fremennik Trials), Watchman 65, Gnome 75. Stalls: Rellekka fur 35 and fish 42, Miscellania vegetables 2 and fish 42.
