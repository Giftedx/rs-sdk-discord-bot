# Mining and smithing

Checked against the server code (vendor/rs-sdk at 3506b20cc). Where this page disagrees with
wiki/skills/mining.md or wiki/skills/smithing.md, this page is correct. The rock locations come
from the map files (`server/content/maps/m*_*.jm2`), read with a script.

## Pickaxes

- The Mining levels on the wiki are correct: bronze 1, iron 1, steel 6, mithril 21, adamant 31, rune 41.
- A pickaxe works from the hand or the inventory. The game uses the best one that your Mining level allows.
- To wield a pickaxe needs Attack: iron 1, steel 5, mithril 20, adamant 30, rune 40.
- The iron pickaxe costs 140 gp at full stock, not 137. Nurmof in the Dwarven Mine sells all six pickaxes. Bob's Axes sells only the bronze one.

## Where the ores are (the wiki gets many wrong)

- Runite: only 5 rocks. 2 in the Wilderness near the Lava Maze (3059, 3885 and 3060, 3884), 1 under the Lava Maze (3046, 10265), and 2 underground near the Druids' Circle (2937, 9882 and 2941, 9884). There is no runite in Al Kharid, Lumbridge Swamp, the Grand Tree or the desert mining camp.
- Adamantite: Al Kharid, and the east side of Lumbridge Swamp (3242, 3165). None at the Fight Arena or inside the Mining Guild.
- Mithril: inside the Mining Guild. None in north Brimhaven or the Crafting Guild.
- Gold: Dwarven Mine (outside the Mining Guild door), Crafting Guild, Brimhaven. None at the Coal Trucks, Barbarian Village or inside the Mining Guild.
- Coal: Mining Guild, Coal Trucks, Barbarian Village. None in SW Varrock or the Crafting Guild.
- Silver: SW Varrock, Al Kharid, Crafting Guild. None in SE Varrock or at the Fight Arena.
- Copper: SE Varrock, Rimmington. None in SW Varrock.
- Iron: SE Varrock, SW Varrock, Al Kharid. None in the Asgarnian Ice Dungeon.
- Clay (level 1), blurite (10) and limestone (10) are also in the game.

## Mining XP per ore

Copper and tin 17.5, iron 35, silver 40, coal 50, gold 65, mithril 80, adamantite 95, runite 125, rune essence 5, clay 5.

## Smithing: bars per item

The wiki says most items take 1 bar. Many take more:

- 1 bar: dagger, axe, mace, med helm, and others.
- 2 bars: scimitar, longsword, full helm, square shield, claws.
- 3 bars: warhammer, battleaxe, chainbody, kiteshield, 2h sword, platelegs, plateskirt.
- 5 bars: platebody.

Smelting XP: bronze 6.2, iron 12.5, silver 13.7, steel 17.5, gold 22.5, mithril 30, adamant 37.5, rune 50. Forging XP is per bar used: bronze 12.5, iron 25, steel 37.5, mithril 50, adamant 62.5, rune 75.
