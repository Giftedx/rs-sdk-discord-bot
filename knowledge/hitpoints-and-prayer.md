# Hitpoints, poison and prayer

Checked against the server code (vendor/rs-sdk at 3506b20cc). Where this page disagrees with
wiki/skills/hitpoints.md or wiki/skills/prayer.md, this page is correct.

## Healing and poison

- There is no prayer that cures poison. The wiki row "Curing prayers" is wrong.
- Poison is cured by antipoison (Herblore 5), superantipoison (Herblore 48), strange fruit, or a death.
- Natural healing is 1 HP every 100 ticks (about 60 seconds). Rapid Heal (Prayer 22) makes it every 50 ticks.
- A monk at the Monastery heals 2 HP plus 20% of your base Hitpoints, for free.

## Death

- You respawn in Lumbridge (3221, 3218).
- You keep 3 items, or 4 with Protect Item. A skulled player keeps 0, or 1 with Protect Item.
- After a death to another player, you respawn at 1 HP.

## Prayer facts the wiki gets wrong

- Brother Jered at the Monastery does not give robes. He only blesses a strung holy symbol. Monk's robes are an item on the ground on the upper floor of the Monastery.
- Burnt bones are not dropped by any NPC. They are only on the ground in some Wilderness areas.
- Against another player, a protect prayer cuts the damage by 40%. Against an NPC, it blocks the hit fully.
- Protect from Magic also stops dragonfire.
- Metal dragons (bronze, iron, steel) also drop dragon bones.
