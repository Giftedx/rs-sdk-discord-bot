# Magic: runes and enchanted jewellery

Checked against the server code (vendor/rs-sdk at 3506b20cc). Where this page disagrees with
wiki/skills/magic.md, this page is correct.

## Which rune each spell type uses

- Bolt spells use chaos runes, not cosmic runes.
- Enchant spells use cosmic runes, not chaos runes.
- The rest of the wiki rune table is correct: mind for strike, body for curse, death for blast, law for teleport, blood for wave, soul for high curses, nature for alchemy.

## Enchanted jewellery

| Enchant level | Item made | Effect |
|---------------|-----------|--------|
| 7 (sapphire) | Ring of recoil | Hits back 10% + 1 of damage, 40 damage in all |
| 7 (sapphire) | Amulet of magic | +10 magic attack |
| 27 (emerald) | Ring of dueling | Teleport to the Duel Arena or Castle Wars, 8 uses |
| 27 (emerald) | Amulet of defence | +7 to all defences |
| 49 (ruby) | Ring of forging | Iron smelting never fails, 140 ores |
| 49 (ruby) | Amulet of strength | +10 strength |
| 57 (diamond) | Ring of life | Teleport at 10% HP or less, 1 use, not above Wilderness level 30 |
| 57 (diamond) | Amulet of power | +6 to all combat stats |
| 68 (dragonstone) | Amulet of glory | +10 attack, +3 defence, +6 strength, +3 prayer, 4 teleport charges, members-only |
| 68 (dragonstone) | Ring of wealth | members-only |

- There is no Amulet of Damned, no Ring of Defence and no Ring of Strength on this server.
- The amulet of glory is not "+6 all combat stats" and is not permanent. The +6 item is the amulet of power.
