Checked against the server code (vendor/rs-sdk at 3506b20cc). Where this page disagrees with
wiki/skills/crafting.md, fletching.md, firemaking.md or herblore.md, this page is correct.

- The wiki rows for oak (level 1) and willow (level 15) are wrong.
- There are no teak logs on this server.
- The tinderbox must be in the inventory. You cannot light a fire on an occupied tile or in a bank.

## Herblore

- There is no Druidic Ritual quest check. Any player can identify herbs at the right level. The quest only gives 250 Herblore XP.
- The option is "Identify", not "Clean". Herblore is members-only.
- There is no Farming skill. Herbs cannot be farmed.
- There is no eyeball and no mistletoe. The secondary is Eye of newt, sold in Taverley.
- Wine of Zamorak is on the ground only in the Asgarnian Chaos Temple (2930, 3515). There is none in the Wilderness.

## Fletching

- Opal bolt tips need Fletching 11, not 17. Pearl bolt tips need 41, not 34.
- Pearl tips come from oyster pearls, not from a gem.
- Fletching darts has no quest check. Only smithing dart tips needs The Tourist Trap, and only part of the way through it.
- Dart fletching levels: bronze 1, iron 22, steel 37, mithril 52, adamant 67, rune 81.
- Only normal logs give arrow shafts (15 per log).

## Crafting

- The studded body needs Crafting 41, not 36. Studded chaps need 44.
- Brother Jered blesses a holy symbol with no Prayer level check.
- Pottery XP comes from two steps: forming on the wheel and firing in the oven. Pie dish: 15 then 10. Bowl: 18 then 15. Pot: 6.3 then 6.3.
- Crafting Guild: needs Crafting 40 and a worn brown apron.
